Questions

Which Macs does SpotPlot run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). SpotPlot isn't available for Windows or iPad yet; an iPad companion app for operators is in the works.

Do I need to be online?

Only to activate your license. After that SpotPlot works without Wi-Fi, and your shows are saved on your Mac.

Can I share a show with my associate or another designer?

Yes. Export the show from its dashboard (or press ⌘E) and send the .spotplot file. Everything comes along: cues, notes, highlights, photos and the logo. They double-click it to open it in SpotPlot.

How do I get updates?

SpotPlot updates itself. You can also choose SpotPlot → Check for Updates at any time. Requires an internet connection and for the app to be open.
