Licenses and Macs

I got a new Mac. How do I move my license?

On the old Mac, open Home → Settings and click Deactivate… next to This Mac, then enter your license key on the new Mac. Old Mac already gone? A Mac that hasn't opened SpotPlot for 30 days frees its spot automatically, or email us and we'll free it right away. To bring your shows along, export each one from the old Mac and import it on the new one (see Sharing).

Does SpotPlot work offline?

Yes. You need an internet connection once, to activate your license. After that your shows are saved on your Mac, so a theatre with no Wi-Fi is fine.

Where do I find my license key?

It's in your purchase email. In the app it's under Home → Settings → Account.

Plans and billing

What's the difference between the plans?

Every plan has every feature. Standard is for working professionals; Student is the same app at a lower price for students with a .edu email (valid for 4 years or graduation date, whichever comes sooner). Production (one flat fee per show, covering the assistant and all operators) and Education (for schools) are coming soon. See Pricing.

How does student verification work?

Buy the Student plan with your .edu email address. We may ask you to confirm it from that address.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly, or cancel?

Yes. Yearly saves 20% on Standard and 25% on Student. You can cancel any time, and SpotPlot keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for.

What happens to my shows if my subscription ends?

Your shows stay on your Mac. Export them as .spotplot files to keep a copy you can open again later.

Using the app

What do the T· numbers mean?

T· is the spot cue number operators use to keep their place, printed under each LQ. Inserting cues makes them jump around; click Renumber T· in the cue list to number them 1, 2, 3… again. Your cue order never changes.

What's the difference between Off and Tracked?

Off means the spot isn't used in that cue. Tracked means nothing changes: the spot carries on from the previous cue.

How do I give the same gel load to every spot?

Fill in one spot's color frames, then on each other spot choose Copy colors from… and pick that spot. The permanent color and fixture type come along too.

I swapped operators between positions. Can I move their cues?

Yes. In Show Settings → Spot Settings, use Swap to exchange all cue data between two spots for the whole show.

How do I hide a detail like intensity from the printouts?

Show Settings → Cue Settings. Untick Show on print (or Show on cue list) for that detail. Nothing is deleted; tick it again to bring it back.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. In the cue list, press ⌘Z.

How do I see the tips again?

Choose Help → Show Tips Again.

Printing

Why are some color frames highlighted in yellow?

Yellow marks a color change: the first time each spot uses a color, and every time it changes.

Can I print just part of the show?

For a spot sheet, set a cue range using T· numbers, and hide Off or Tracked cues. Leave the range blank to print everything.

Troubleshooting

SpotPlot won't open, or macOS says it can't check it.

Make sure you dragged SpotPlot into your Applications folder and opened it from there, not from the disk image. SpotPlot is signed and notarized by Apple. If macOS still won't open it, email us with a screenshot of the message.

The print preview is blank or says it can't make the PDF.

Quit SpotPlot and open it again. If it keeps happening, email us your SpotPlot version and which sheet you were printing.

A .spotplot file won't import.

Make sure you're on the latest version (SpotPlot → Check for Updates). Files made by a newer version need the newer app. Still stuck? Email us the file.

How do I update SpotPlot?

SpotPlot updates itself. Choose SpotPlot → Check for Updates, or Home → Settings → Check Now, to check now, or download the latest version and replace the app in Applications. Your shows aren't affected.
