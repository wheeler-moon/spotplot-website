Plans and prices

SpotPlot for Mac is sold as a subscription, monthly or yearly. Plans and prices are on the Pricing page, in US dollars. Sales tax or VAT is added where the law requires it, and the total is shown before you pay. Every plan includes every feature.

Automatic renewal

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year, and your card is charged the plan's then-current price, until you cancel. When you subscribe you'll be asked to agree to this, and you'll get a confirmation email with your plan, price, renewal schedule and how to cancel. For yearly plans we'll email you a reminder before each renewal.

If a payment fails, we'll let you know and try again for a short time. If it still can't be collected, your subscription ends.

Canceling

You can cancel any time, online: in SpotPlot, open Settings and click Manage… next to Subscription, or use the link in any receipt email. You can also email wheeler@wheelermoon.com. Canceling stops future charges; SpotPlot keeps working until the end of the period you've already paid for. You can switch between monthly and yearly the same way.

Refunds

All payments are final and aren't refunded, including for any unused part of a month or year, except where the law requires a refund. Canceling stops future charges (see Canceling). If you were charged by mistake, for example twice for the same period, email us and we'll correct it.

Price changes

If a plan's price goes up, we'll email you at least 30 days before it applies to your subscription, so you can cancel first if you'd like. A new price applies from your next renewal after that notice.

Student plan

The Student plan is for students enrolled at a school or college. Buy it with your school (.edu) email address; we may ask you to confirm it from that address. It lasts for up to 4 years or until you graduate, whichever comes first, after which you can move to Standard. Using the Student plan without being eligible may end it.
