Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

This policy covers SpotPlot for Mac, SpotPlot for iPad and spotplotapp.com, made by The Wheeler Moon Group in New York ("we", "us"). It explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • Your shows stay on your devices. We never see them.
  • We keep only what we need to sell and manage your license: your email, your plan and license key, and the Macs using it.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card number.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies on this website. We don't sell or share your information for marketing.

Your shows

Everything you put into SpotPlot (shows, cues, characters, notes, photos, logos) is saved on your Mac. When you share a show as a .spotplot file, or send it to SpotPlot for iPad, it goes only where you send it: the iPad link works over your own local network, directly between your devices. None of it passes through our servers.

What we collect

When you buy a plan

Your name and email address, the plan you chose and your billing details. Stripe collects and stores your payment card; we see only things like the card brand, its last four digits and whether a payment went through. If you choose the Student plan we may ask you to confirm your school email address.

To activate and manage your license

SpotPlot for Mac sends our license server your license key, a random ID that SpotPlot creates for that Mac, and the SpotPlot version. The random ID isn't your Mac's serial number or name and can't identify you on its own. We keep a record of the Macs using your license and the date each was last used, so we can confirm your license and let you move it to a new Mac. Like most servers, ours also sees the internet address a request comes from.

When you contact us

Whatever you choose to send: your email address and message, and any screenshots or files you attach. SpotPlot's Send Feedback and Contact Support start an email with your SpotPlot version, macOS version and Mac type filled in; you can see and edit all of it before you send.

Updates

SpotPlot for Mac checks for new versions through update.electronjs.org, which receives your SpotPlot version and the type of Mac it runs on so it can offer the right download.

How we use it

Only to provide SpotPlot: to process your purchase, issue and manage your license, keep it to the Macs you use, answer your questions, send receipts and messages about your account (such as a renewal reminder or a failed payment), and prevent misuse of licenses. We don't use it for advertising and we don't sell it.

If you're in the EU or UK: we rely on what's needed to perform our contract with you (your purchase and license), our legitimate interest in preventing license misuse, and legal obligations such as keeping tax records.

Services we use

These companies handle information for us, only to run SpotPlot:

  • Stripe: payments, receipts and the page where you manage your subscription. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Render: hosts our license server.
  • GitHub: hosts this website and the SpotPlot download.
  • Our email provider: for messages you send us and our replies.

Some of these are based in the United States, so your information may be processed there. We may also share information if the law requires it, or with a buyer if SpotPlot is ever sold, under this same policy.

SpotPlot for iPad

SpotPlot for iPad doesn't collect anything. It has no account and no analytics. Shows you add are saved on the iPad. It talks only to SpotPlot on your own Mac, over your local network, after you enter that show's code; that link is how it gets show updates. Flags you add to cues (Missed, Clarify, Idea) stay on the iPad unless you share them yourself.

This website

spotplotapp.com has no analytics, no ads and sets no cookies. Its fonts and images are served from the site itself. GitHub, which hosts it, keeps standard technical logs (such as internet addresses) to run and protect the service.

How long we keep it

Your account and license records for as long as your license exists, plus as long as tax and accounting rules require us to keep purchase records afterward (usually up to seven years). A Mac you remove from your license (Settings → Deactivate) is taken off it right away. Support emails for as long as they're useful for helping you.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, by emailing wheeler@wheelermoon.com. Deleting your account ends your license. We'll answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), you may have further rights, such as receiving a copy of your information or objecting to how we use it, and you can complain to your local data protection authority. We don't sell or "share" personal information as California law defines it.

Children

SpotPlot is made for theatre professionals and students. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters to how we use your information, we'll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
